# Light Health Demo Script

## Opening Statement
"Light Health is an AI-powered platform that helps teams proactively manage customer health and predict churn risk. Let me walk you through how it works."

---

## 1. **The Problem** (30 seconds)
- Customer success teams manage dozens or hundreds of accounts manually
- Hard to prioritize which accounts need attention
- Lack of real-time visibility into account health
- Reactive instead of proactive — find problems after they happen

---

## 2. **The Upload Flow** (1 minute)
*Navigate to: Upload page*

- Start with a simple spreadsheet of your customer data
- Light Health accepts standard formats (.xlsx, .xls)
- Show the template with key fields: Company Name, Industry, Contract Value, Renewal Date, etc.
- Click "Import & generate profiles"

**Key message:** "No complex integrations needed. Just upload your data."

---

## 3. **AI-Generated Profiles** (1-2 minutes)
*Navigate to: Profiles page*

- Light Health automatically analyzes each company using AI
- Generates an AI-drafted profile with key insights
- Shows account health status: healthy, warning, or critical
- Displays renewal dates and validation status
- Teams review and validate before flags go live

**Key message:** "AI does the research so your team doesn't have to. They just validate."

**Show specific examples:**
- Green flag = healthy account (validated)
- Red/Amber flags = needs attention (review needed)
- Confidence levels for each assessment

---

## 4. **The Overview Dashboard** (1-2 minutes)
*Navigate to: Overview page*

- Bird's-eye view of all accounts
- Quick filter by health status (red, amber, green)
- Summary metrics: Total ARR tracked, ARR at risk, Account health breakdown
- Data table showing top flags, renewal dates, CSM owner
- "View" link drills into individual account details

**Key message:** "One screen shows everything. Instantly spot accounts that need action."

---

## 5. **Daily Briefing** (1 minute)
*Navigate to: Today page (if data loads)*

- Daily digest of the most important account health changes
- Actionable summary: what changed today, which accounts need attention
- Organized by priority: critical → warning → healthy

**Key message:** "Every morning, your team gets a focused briefing instead of sifting through reports."

---

## 6. **The Value Prop** (30 seconds)
- **Speed:** AI research + validation = days instead of weeks per account
- **Scale:** Handle hundreds of accounts without proportional team growth
- **Proactive:** Spot churn risk early, take action before it's too late
- **Accuracy:** Human validation + AI insights = confidence in every flag

---

## 7. **Closing**
"Light Health turns customer health management from a manual, reactive process into an AI-powered, proactive system. Teams can focus on strategic work instead of data gathering."

---

## Optional Demo Points
If asked about specific features:
- **Renewal tracking:** Shows days until renewal, urgency level
- **CSM ownership:** Visible throughout platform for accountability
- **Validation workflow:** Prevents bad data from reaching stakeholders
- **Flexible updates:** Easy to re-upload as data changes
